Frequently Asked Questions

How do I place an order?

Press ORDER NOW on any package. WhatsApp opens with a message that already names the service and package.

Are domain and hosting included in website packages?

No. Domain and recurring hosting charges may be separate. The Premium website package includes hosting setup assistance.

Is the advertising budget included in Paid Promotion?

No. Your advertising budget is separate from our service fees. We do not guarantee views, followers or sales.

How many revisions do I get?

Video editing packages include 1, 2 or 3 revisions depending on the package you choose.

Ready to start your project?

Tell us what you are creating and we will suggest the right package.
